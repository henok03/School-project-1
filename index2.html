About us

Who We Are

Haile Mariyam Mamo High School is a leading educational institution dedicated to nurturing bright minds and shaping future leaders. It was founded in 1934 during the reign of Emperor Haile Selassie and the reason to start this school is the head of the municipality, Gubre Agaez, and the head of Abnet School, Teklegiorgis Kidane, requested the opening of a modern school in Debre Bierhan to the Emperor Especially, for orphans, disabled eunuchs, and children of veterans.

They announced the good news. This royal motto was the starting point for the Hailemariam Mamo School. The king, in response to a public request and a promise he made, selected a building to house the school. The current Hailemariam Mamo School was chosen. There were several reasons why Seferaw was chosen as the school. The main reason was that it was a self-contained compound with enough buildings. This compound was the headquarters of the Italian army and the main ammunition depot. It housed the residences of various officials, including the commander-in-chief: a primary school (1-4); and a Catholic church.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

Our mission is to cultivate an inclusive and rigorous academic environment that empowers students from all backgrounds—including those traditionally underserved—to achieve excellence. We are committed to nurturing the intellectual, emotional, and social competence of every learner by providing a modern, high-quality education rooted in integrity and discipline.

Vision

Haile Mariam Mamo High School aspires to be a premier center of educational excellence in Ethiopia, recognized for transforming lives through the power of knowledge. We envision a community where every graduate emerges as a responsible citizen, a lifelong learner, and a positive contributor to society. Our goal is to bridge the gap between tradition and innovation, ensuring that our legacy of serving veterans' children and the underprivileged continues through the production of future leaders who are ethically grounded and globally competitive.
