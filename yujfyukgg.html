Our Story

Haile Mariam Mamo School was founded in 1998 with a vision to provide world-class education rooted in Ethiopian values while embracing global standards of academic excellence. Our school has grown from humble beginnings to become one of the most respected educational institutions in the region.

Our Mission

To provide transformative education that develops intellectually curious, ethically grounded, and globally competent citizens who contribute positively to society.

We believe that every child deserves an exceptional education that nurtures their unique talents and prepares them for success in an ever-changing world. Our dedicated faculty, comprehensive curriculum, and state-of-the-art facilities create an environment where every student can thrive academically, socially, and personally.

Our Values

Excellence

We pursue the highest standards in all aspects of education and personal development.

Inclusion

Every student matters and belongs in our diverse and welcoming community.

Innovation

We embrace creativity and forward-thinking approaches to education and learning.

Service

We develop compassionate leaders who serve others and make a positive impact in their communities.
